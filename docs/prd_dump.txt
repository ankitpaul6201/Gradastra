1. Executive Summary
Gradastra is an independent global education advisory platform designed to help students make informed decisions about universities and colleges before applying or enrolling. It combines independently researched university information, structured student reviews, and free guidance from verified senior students who have first-hand experience at the institutions they discuss.
Unlike commission-driven admission consultancies, Gradastra will not maintain commercial partnerships, admission agreements or referral arrangements with universities or colleges. It will not receive commissions for student admissions or sell student leads. Its purpose is not to persuade students to join a particular institution; it is to help them assess whether an institution is right for their goals, circumstances and budget.
Core promise: Real colleges. Real students. Unfiltered experiences. Informed decisions. Always free.

5. Student-to-Student Advisory Model
5.1 Advisor eligibility
Be currently enrolled at a recognized college or university.
Normally be in the third year or above of an undergraduate program, or an eligible stage of postgraduate study.
Submit evidence of current enrolment for private verification.
Complete advisor onboarding and agree to platform policies.
Disclose conflicts of interest and distinguish personal experience from hearsay.
Avoid fabricated claims, guaranteed outcomes or undisclosed promotional activity.
5.2 Verification workflow
Applicant creates an advisor account and selects country, institution, program and current academic stage.
Applicant submits a suitable enrolment document or student ID through a secure upload flow.
Admin reviews evidence and records a verification decision.
Approved advisors receive a verified-student status badge.
Advisor publishes a profile describing their experience and topics they can discuss.
Verification is periodically refreshed; expired or uncertain status is clearly labelled.
5.3 Advisor profile and topics
A profile may include institution, campus, program, academic stage, country, experience areas, languages, availability and consultation formats. Public profiles must not expose student IDs, private contact details or verification documents.
Teaching quality and faculty accessibility.
Curriculum, workload, examination patterns and academic support.
Internships, placement experiences and career support.
Labs, libraries, classrooms and other infrastructure.
Campus culture, clubs, events and student communities.
Hostels, accommodation, food, transport and living expenses.
Administrative support and international-student services.
Advantages, disadvantages and advice the advisor would give a prospective student.
Advisors must make clear whether a statement is a personal experience, a second-hand account or an official published fact. A single student's experience must not be represented as the universal experience of an institution.
5.4 Compensation and participation
Student consultations are free. The initial model is voluntary participation, with optional non-financial recognition such as contribution records or certificates. Recognition must not depend on recommending a particular institution or producing admissions.
No consultation fees charged to students.
No paid leads or referral fees.
No commissions or incentives for recommending particular universities.
No paid recommendations or preferential ranking.
No requirement for advisors to disclose private phone numbers or email addresses.

6.3 Student Reviews
Students can submit structured reviews for institutions and programs. Review dimensions may include academics, faculty, infrastructure, placements and internships, campus environment, accommodation, food and facilities, administration and overall experience.
Capture institution, program, campus where relevant, student status and review date.
Allow written experience, positive aspects, challenges and advice for applicants.
Support private identity verification while allowing a public display name or pseudonym.
Provide report, moderation, correction and appeal mechanisms.
Do not remove legitimate criticism solely because it is negative; act on policy violations and demonstrably false or unlawful content through a fair process.
6.4 Ask a Senior
The signature feature connects prospective applicants with current senior students.
Student opens a university profile and selects Talk to a Senior.
Student browses eligible advisors by program, academic stage, expertise, language and availability.
Student submits a consultation request with their questions and preferred time.
Advisor accepts, declines or proposes another time.
The parties communicate through platform messaging or an approved meeting link.
Student can submit feedback after the consultation.
Initial formats: text consultation, scheduled voice call using an external meeting link, group Q&A and anonymous question submission. Built-in video calling can be considered later.
6.5 College Comparison
Allow comparison of two to four institutions across tuition, living costs, academic information, placements, faculty feedback, infrastructure, accommodation, international support and student satisfaction. Do not declare a universal winner; explain trade-offs against the applicant's stated priorities.
6.6 Country Education Guides
Potential country coverage includes India, United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Ireland, Singapore, Netherlands, Japan, South Korea and New Zealand, with expansion as data quality and contributor coverage allow.
Education system and institution types.
Tuition ranges and cost-of-living estimates.
Admission process and language requirements.
Scholarships and funding opportunities.
Official visa and immigration resources.
Work regulations for international students, linked to official sources.
Accommodation, transport and cultural considerations.
Visa and immigration content must link to official government sources, show a last-updated date and avoid presenting the platform as a legal authority.
6.7 Personalized College Shortlisting
Students can enter academic qualifications, grades/GPA, preferred subjects and countries, budget, scholarship needs, language proficiency and career goals. The platform can produce an explainable shortlist showing why each institution may fit, published eligibility requirements, affordability considerations, disadvantages and missing information. It must not guarantee admission, scholarships or employment.
6.8 Student Community
A moderated discussion area can cover universities, countries, scholarships, admission experiences, campus life, accommodation, placements and international student experiences. Include reporting, blocking, anti-spam and moderation tools.

11. Independence and Business Rules
The following are foundational rules and should be reflected in policy, product design and operating practice:
No university or college partnership agreements that influence coverage or recommendations.
No admission commissions, referral fees or payment tied to student enrolment.
No paid university rankings or paid recommendation placement.
No sale of student leads to admission agents or institutions.
No hidden promotional placements.
No charges to students for consultations.
No fabricated reviews or undisclosed sponsored experiences.
No suppression of legitimate criticism to protect an institution's reputation.
Public independence statement
Gradastra is an independent, student-focused education advisory platform. We do not have commercial partnerships, admission agreements or referral arrangements with universities or colleges. We do not receive commissions for student admissions. University information is gathered from publicly available sources, verified records and student-reported experiences. Our objective is to help students make informed decisions through transparent and independent guidance.
Independence does not mean every statement is automatically verified. Gradastra must label sources, dates, evidence and confidence appropriately.

15. Trust, Safety and Data Protection
HTTPS throughout the platform.
Secure authentication, role-based authorization and server-side permission checks.
Private storage for student verification documents, with restricted access.
Encryption in transit and at rest where supported by the selected services.
Data minimization, retention schedules and deletion workflows.
Consent before publishing profiles, reviews, photos or testimonials.
Report, block, moderation, appeal and account suspension processes.
Rate limiting, spam prevention and abuse monitoring.
No public display of student IDs, enrolment documents or private contact details.
Allow pseudonymous public reviews where appropriate while privately verifying eligibility. Establish fair procedures for responding to complaints about allegedly false or defamatory content. Seek qualified legal advice for launch jurisdictions and cross-border data handling.

21. Business Model and Sustainability
Gradastra will remain free for students. Initial operations may be founder-funded or supported by resources that do not compromise editorial independence. Potential future sources to evaluate include voluntary donations, unrestricted grants and carefully screened non-commercial educational support.
Any future funding source must be publicly disclosed. No funding arrangement may influence reviews, rankings, university coverage or student recommendations. If sponsorship is ever introduced, it must be clearly labelled, separated from editorial decisions and governed by a published policy.

23. Additional Product Requirements
23.1 University information credibility
Each university profile should show source availability, last verification date, number of student reviews, verified contributor coverage and information completeness. Information completeness must not be confused with university quality; a university with fewer reviews should not automatically receive a lower quality rating.
23.2 Advisor quality
Track consultation completion, response time, student feedback, helpfulness and policy compliance. Do not reward advisors for recommending particular institutions or producing admissions.
23.3 Editorial independence
Publish a methodology page explaining data collection, student verification, review moderation, rating calculations, update cycles, correction requests, complaint handling and funding disclosures.
23.4 Legal and operational preparation
Terms of Service.
Privacy Policy.
Student Advisor Agreement.
Community Guidelines.
Review and Moderation Policy.
Educational guidance and admissions disclaimer.
Data retention and deletion policy.
Consent and cross-border privacy procedures.
Obtain qualified legal review for the countries in which Gradastra operates. Do not claim accreditation or guarantee admissions, scholarships, visas, placements or employment.
24. Final Development Priorities
The first release should focus on five capabilities:
A professional global education website.
A searchable university directory with genuinely useful information.
Verified third-year-and-above student advisors, with equivalent postgraduate eligibility.
Independent student reviews and university analysis.
A free consultation request and management system.
The largest early challenge is not simply building the website. It is creating a trustworthy information base and recruiting enough verified senior students to make the platform useful. Begin with a focused set of institutions, prove the verification and consultation workflows, and expand based on evidence.
25. Recommended Decisions Before Development
