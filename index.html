What is Gradastra?

Gradastra is a 100% free, independent college intelligence platform connecting prospective undergraduate and graduate students directly with verified senior students studying at universities worldwide. Gradastra accepts 0% university commission, ensuring 100% uncompromised, student-aligned guidance.

Why Choose Gradastra Over Traditional Admission Agents?

  • Zero Commission: No university kickbacks or hidden partner quotas.
  • Real Seniors, Not Sales Agents: 1-on-1 discussions with verified students currently enrolled on campus.
  • Unfiltered Campus Realities: Ground truths on workloads, housing quality, faculty accessibility, and living costs.
  • Authentic Placement Statistics: Transparent median salaries and realistic international placement figures.
